USNVDAEarnings

Nvidia guides to $108bn while assuming no China revenue at all

Rev $96.2bn +106% y/y Data Center $89.0bn +117% y/y GM 75.0% EPS $2.46 GAAP

Q2 FY2027 (quarter ended 26 Jul) beat on every line, driven by the Blackwell Ultra ramp; hyperscale revenue more than doubled year over year. Q3 guidance of $108bn assumes zero China compute. CFO Colette Kress and Jensen Huang pointed to roughly 70% revenue growth in fiscal 2028, far above consensus.

Why it mattersThe cleanest read on the AI capex cycle, and the guide is struck with China at zero — so any policy thaw is upside not yet in the model. Shares rose about 6% the next session and pulled the Nasdaq up roughly 1% with them.

Alibaba raises $10.2bn for AI — and drops more than 10% doing it

Size HK$80bn Shares 710m @ HK$112.70 Dilution ≈3.6% Closed 26 Aug

The largest follow-on ever by a Hong Kong-listed company and the third largest globally in 2026, behind only Alphabet and Intel. Every dollar is earmarked for AI: about 60% (HK$47.9bn) to expand global compute infrastructure, about 40% (HK$31.9bn) to hyperscale AI data centres and an Agentic Cloud upgrade of legacy cloud.

Why it mattersA conviction signal on AI demand paid for with equity rather than cash flow — which is exactly why the stock fell. It also dragged the whole Hong Kong tape: the Hang Seng TECH index was down 3.86% at Monday midday, with Alibaba the single biggest contributor.
SCMP · also Alibaba 6-K, SEC

Monetary policy Next decision16 Sep 2026

Sticky inflation has taken the September cut off the table — and put a hike on it

The Fed held at 3.50–3.75% on 29 July. Its August forecast has PCE inflation running at 3.6%, well above the 2% target. As of 12 August, futures put roughly 40% on a September hike and 60% on a hold. Jackson Hole fell inside this scan window.

Why it mattersLong-duration tech multiples are the first thing repriced when the terminal rate drifts up. The AI earnings story is intact; the discount rate applied to it is the risk.
Forbes · 12 Aug
Export controls StatusTightened May 2026

China is currently an option on Nvidia’s numbers, not a line in them

A January 2026 Commerce rule reopened China to Nvidia’s H200 and AMD’s MI325X. Then in late May, Commerce moved to restrict the most advanced parts — Rubin, Blackwell, MI350x — from Chinese-headquartered entities located outside China, closing the offshore-subsidiary route. Licensing now follows the parent company, not the address.

Why it mattersNvidia’s $108bn guide assumes zero China compute, so the restriction is already fully discounted. That makes the policy asymmetric for holders: relaxation is upside, further tightening costs nothing that was being counted.
CNBC · 31 May
Trade truce Cliff date10 Nov 2026

The tariff truce holds, on a clock

Fentanyl duties were cut from 20% to 10%, taking the overall rate on Chinese imports from 41% to 31%, and the 24% reciprocal duties are suspended until 10 November 2026. China in turn suspended its October 2025 export controls on rare earths, gallium, germanium, antimony and graphite, issuing general licences for US end users. Analysts read the arrangement as a tactical truce rather than a strategic shift.

Why it mattersDiary 10 November. Both sides retained their leverage, so the expiry is a live repricing event for anything with a China supply chain or a China revenue line.
